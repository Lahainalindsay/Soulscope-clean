# Canon and Constellation Bible Relationship

## What the Canon Governs

`docs/SOULSCOPE_CANON.md` and its immutable source PDF govern:

- product truth
- scientific and ethical boundaries
- permitted and prohibited claims
- product vocabulary
- visual laws
- governance principles
- the current voice-first production boundary
- the rule that the instrument provides evidence and the individual provides meaning

## What the Constellation Bible Governs

`docs/CONSTELLATION_BIBLE.md` and its immutable source docx govern:

- detailed operational constellation system
- dimensions and permanent IDs
- evidence relationships
- state geometry and seed states
- blends and unresolved behavior
- typed interactions
- confidence and missingness handling
- prompt transitions and baseline hierarchy
- narrative interpretation boundaries
- renderer input specifications
- calibration notes, validation program, and registry governance

## Where They Overlap

Both documents constrain:

- voice as the current production observation layer
- non-diagnostic reflective language
- traceability to measured evidence
- uncertainty preservation
- visual truth requirements
- prohibited hidden-truth, deception, spiritual-measurement, organ-health, identity, and personality claims

When they overlap, the Canon controls product truth and prohibited claims. The Bible controls operational detail only where it does not conflict with the Canon.

## Where the Bible Adds Operational Detail

The Bible adds:

- the four constellations: COG, REG, CAP, EXP
- the sixteen permanent point IDs
- seed state IDs such as COG-017, REG-022, CAP-012, and EXP-009
- candidate-state selection sequence
- baseline hierarchy separating within-scan baseline, personal Reference Signature, and population priors
- normalization and evidence-family aggregation language
- machine-readable registry blueprints
- validation and ground-truth strategy

## Conflicts

No direct Canon/Bible conflict was identified in this documentation task.

Potential future conflicts must be documented before implementation when:

- a Bible threshold or formula exceeds validated acoustic evidence
- a visual mapping makes unsupported meaning appear certain
- a narrative template could imply diagnosis, hidden truth, personality, spirituality, or organ health
- a future modality is treated as production-ready before Canon governance permits it

## Unresolved Interpretation Questions

- How much of the Bible's machine-readable registry blueprint is normative versus illustrative?
- Which source thresholds are ready for implementation and which require calibration studies first?
- How should table and image content from the `.docx` be represented in Markdown if human review finds extraction loss?
- Which validation phase gates must pass before the Bible becomes implementation-ready?
