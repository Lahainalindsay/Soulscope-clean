The Instrument
       A scope is an instrument that helps us see what is already present, but
                           ordinarily difficult to perceive.


SoulScope is an instrument for observing the inner environment. It is designed to organize measurable biological
and behavioral signals into information a person can use for reflection, self-understanding, and awareness of
change over time.
The first generation is intentionally focused on voice. Future layers may include eye behavior, pupil dilation,
facial and body expression, breathing, heart rate, movement, sleep, and other measurable signals. These are
part of the long-term architecture, not claims about the present product.

  C U R R E N T    P R O D U C T       B O U N D A R Y

  Voice is the first and only production observation layer being perfected now. Future modalities remain research
  directions until they meet the same evidence, privacy, reliability, and validation standards.




A Letter from the Founder
Every instrument humanity has created has expanded what we can perceive. A microscope reveals cells. A
telescope reveals distant galaxies. A stethoscope reveals rhythms inside the body. A scope does not create
something new; it helps us observe what was already there.
SoulScope began with the belief that our inner world is always expressing itself. Voice, breathing, eyes, posture,
movement, facial expression, heart rhythms, and many other signals continually shift as we think, adapt, recover,
relate, and move through life. Most of these changes are too subtle to consciously notice. Together they form
patterns.
My personal inspiration also comes from faith: the belief that creation begins with the spoken word, and that
voice and vibration are fundamental expressions of life. This spiritual inspiration explains why voice feels so
central to the vision. It does not replace the scientific boundary of the product. SoulScope measures acoustic
and behavioral signals; it does not claim to measure the soul, spirituality, divine activity, identity, or hidden truth.
The voice is the first lens because it is immediate, accessible, richly structured, and inseparable from the body
that produces it. SoulScope exists to help make patterns in that expression visible, while preserving uncertainty,
context, and the individual’s authority over meaning.


       The instrument provides evidence. The individual provides meaning.

SoulScope does not map one acoustic feature to one emotion. Vocal measures are many-to-many cues
influenced by anatomy, language, task, device, health, environment, style, and context. Every abstraction must
therefore remain linked to its source measurements and reliability conditions.

3. Normalization and Confidence
When a compatible trusted reference exists, use robust median/MAD normalization:
                                 z_f = clamp((x_f - median_ref) / max(1.4826 × MAD_ref, ε_f), -3, 3)

If the reference is absent, incompatible, version-mismatched, or has zero spread, personal deviation remains
unresolved. Missing baseline is unknown, not population average.

1. Brand Definition
             SoulScope is an instrument for observing the inner environment.


The brand sits between scientific instrumentation and intimate self-reflection. It should never feel clinical,
mystical, generic wellness, cyberpunk, or emotionally manipulative. The visual and verbal system must
communicate precision without coldness, beauty without decoration, and possibility without certainty.

Brand attributes
• Instrument-like, evidence-led, and traceable.
• Premium, quiet, editorial, and spacious.
• Curious rather than declarative.
• Personal without becoming invasive.
• Scientific without resembling a hospital product.
• Mysterious enough to invite discovery, clear enough to earn trust.

1. Architectural Invariants
• The Evidence Ledger is the only inference gateway.
• Narrative and visualization consume the same immutable completed result object.
• The renderer never analyzes raw audio or recalculates dimensions.
• Permanent IDs are never recycled or silently repurposed.
• Every production result stores extractor, registry, rule, model, and renderer versions.
• Missing evidence remains missing; it is never silently imputed as neutral.
• No generative model performs scoring, state selection, causal inference, or unsupported interpretation.

  backups must not be tracked.

• Visual renderer changes require fixture exports and a renderer version bump when geometry materially changes.

  notes.

  approved storage policy.

2. Ground-Truth Strategy
• Do not use one mood questionnaire as “truth.” Use task condition, user reflection, repeated measurements,
  observer ratings where appropriate, and governed physiological measures.
• Separate expressed affect from felt affect. Voice can communicate an expression that differs from subjective
  experience.
• Pre-register primary hypotheses, feature families, thresholds, exclusions, and evaluation metrics.
• Use participant-level splits; never place the same speaker in train and test sets.
• Report calibration, uncertainty, subgroup performance, false-positive costs, missingness, and abstention—not only
  accuracy or AUC.
• Validate display names for comprehension, emotional impact, unintended judgment, and cultural interpretation.

4. Research Sources and Intellectual Position
SoulScope adopts standardized acoustic vocabulary, robust personal baselines, many-to-many cue
interpretation, abstention, uncertainty, segmentation, temporal change, and traceable reporting. It rejects
proprietary score cloning, one-cue/one-emotion logic, deception claims, diagnostic authority, closed-corpus
certainty, and feature-count marketing.
• eGeMAPS and openSMILE concepts for standardized feature vocabulary and reproducible extraction.
• Praat/Parselmouth for validated F0, formant, jitter, shimmer, HNR/CPP workflows with quality gates.
• Vocal emotion and stress literature for bounded many-to-many cue interpretation.
• Voice-health literature for research potential, longitudinal methods, privacy, fairness, and clinical-translation
  cautions.
• LVA and EmotionLogic as architectural references for calibration, segmentation, temporal aggregation, and
  structured reporting—not proprietary algorithm replication or truth/deception claims.
