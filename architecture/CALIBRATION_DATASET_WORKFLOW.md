# Calibration Dataset Workflow

Status: RESEARCH FOUNDATION ONLY  
Production inference changed: **No**

This workflow is the single path from today's abstaining Dimension engine toward validated results. It deliberately does not add a second inference backend.

## Boundary

Production remains:

`MeasurementRecord -> Evidence Ledger -> Dimension Result -> abstain when calibration is unavailable`.

Calibration work is offline/research-only until a future version passes the promotion gate in `DIMENSION_CALIBRATION_REQUIREMENTS.md`.

## Inclusion gate

A scan may enter a calibration dataset only when:

- the scan is finalized;
- the MeasurementRecord is qualified and semantically eligible;
- the Evidence Ledger is complete;
- the Dimension result completed (including a scientifically correct abstention);
- Evidence and Dimension engine/registry versions exactly match the dataset contract.

Deleted, partial, queued, legacy-version, and incompatible rows are excluded rather than silently mixed.

## Dataset manifest

Every frozen dataset must have a manifest containing:

- dataset ID and manifest version;
- exact engine/rule/registry versions;
- pseudonymous participant IDs and scan IDs;
- inclusion policy version;
- participant and scan counts;
- participant-separated train/calibration/final-evaluation requirement;
- source-to-Evidence-to-Dimension provenance policy;
- explicit preservation of missing/rejected/contradicted/insufficient/unavailable states;
- `RESEARCH_ONLY_UNVALIDATED` scientific status;
- `productionScoringAuthorized: false`.

No labels, directionality, weights, thresholds, confidence, posterior values, or psychological interpretations are generated by this layer.

## What produces useful results next

1. Collect repeated, consent-compatible scans under the fixed protocol and current versions.
2. Freeze versioned research datasets using this inclusion gate.
3. Validate D1 descriptive-functional dimensions first because they can be tested against repeatability and prompt-contrast behavior without inventing psychological labels.
4. For D2 dimensions, predefine independent external targets before fitting anything. Never derive the target from the same voice features being evaluated.
5. Keep REG-P4, CAP-P2, and EXP-P4 abstaining until the protocol actually observes recovery, reserve, and interaction.
6. Split by participant before model development. A participant's repeated scans may never leak into final evaluation.
7. Pre-register metrics and numeric acceptance thresholds before final evaluation.
8. Promote only a new version that passes the existing calibration promotion gate. Historical results remain immutable.

## User-facing product strategy

Insight should come from validated observations, not dramatic labels. The eventual narrative layer should translate publishable findings into plain language and preserve uncertainty. A result can be compelling by showing repeatable organization, continuity, range, mobilization, stability, or short-interval sustainability patterns and how they changed across prompt contexts. It must not claim emotion, diagnosis, personality, deception, identity, causality, or an unobserved hidden state.

The three daily-life examples and balancing question belong downstream of validated Dimension findings. Until a finding is publishable, that section must remain unavailable rather than filling the gap with generated intuition.
